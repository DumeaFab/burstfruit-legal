Privacy Policy

Effective date: April 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how BurstFruit ("we", "us", or "our") handles information when you use our mobile application BurstFruit (the "App").

1. Information We Collect

We do not collect any personally identifiable information such as your name, email address, phone number, or location.

The App stores game progress data (high scores, settings, achievements) locally on your device only using AsyncStorage. This data never leaves your device and is not transmitted to us.

2. Advertising

BurstFruit uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect and use certain information to serve personalized ads, including:

  • Advertising ID (IDFA on iOS, GAID on Android)
  • Device information (model, OS version)
  • General location (country/region)
  • App usage data

On iOS, we request your permission before accessing the Advertising ID, in compliance with Apple's App Tracking Transparency (ATT) framework. You may choose to deny tracking, in which case only non-personalized ads will be shown.

For more information on how Google uses this data, please visit: Google Privacy Policy

To opt out of personalized advertising by Google: Google Ad Settings

3. Children's Privacy

BurstFruit is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us.

4. Data Retention

All game data is stored locally on your device. You can delete this data at any time by uninstalling the App. We do not retain any user data on our servers.

5. Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party services:

  • Google AdMob – Advertising (Privacy Policy)

6. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights regarding your data under GDPR (EU), CCPA (California), or other applicable laws. Since we do not collect personal data directly, most of these rights apply to data held by our advertising partner, Google AdMob.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by updating the effective date at the top of this page.
